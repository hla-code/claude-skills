# Pre-Approved Contractual Replacement Clauses

## 1. Client Dependency & Schedule Adjustment Clause
> **Customer Obligations & Timeline Adjustment**: "Timely performance by Provider is contingent upon Customer providing required data, credentials, and approvals as specified in Section 4. In the event of a Customer-caused delay exceeding two (2) business days, all subsequent milestone delivery dates shall be extended automatically on a day-for-day basis, and Provider reserves the right to reallocate staffing resources accordingly."

## 2. Deemed Acceptance Clause
> **Review and Acceptance Period**: "Customer shall have five (5) business days following receipt of each deliverable ('Review Period') to verify conformance against the Acceptance Criteria. Customer shall provide written notice of acceptance or an itemized written notice of non-conformance. If Customer fails to provide written notification within the Review Period or puts the deliverable into productive/operational use, the deliverable shall be deemed accepted in full."

## 3. Pre-Existing IP / Background Rights Carve-Out
> **Background Technology**: "Provider retains all right, title, and interest in and to its pre-existing intellectual property, reusable software modules, templates, methodologies, and general algorithms ('Background IP'). To the extent Background IP is embedded in any final Deliverable, Provider grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use such Background IP solely in connection with Customer's internal use of the Deliverable."

## 4. Change Control Clause
> **Scope Changes**: "Any change to the Scope, Deliverables, Timeline, or Assumptions herein must be documented in a written Change Order signed by authorized representatives of both parties. Provider is under no obligation to commence work on requested changes until such Change Order is executed."
