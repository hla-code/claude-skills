# Statement of Work: Enterprise Marketing Mix Modeling Implementation

**Client**: Global Retail Corp  
**Provider**: DataSense Consulting LLC  
**Fee**: Fixed Fee $95,000  
**Timeline**: 8 Weeks  

### 1. Scope of Work
Provider will ingest, clean, and model all historical marketing data across 14 channels (digital, TV, print, out-of-home) and deliver a fully calibrated Meridian MMM model, executive dashboard, and strategic optimization recommendations. Provider will provide continuous support and revisions until Client is fully satisfied with model accuracy.

### 2. Deliverables
1. Data Preparation Pipeline: Cleaned dataset combining client CRM, ad spend, and organic sales.
2. Meridian MMM Model: Calibrated model providing ROAS estimates by channel.
3. Training: Provider will train all Client analysts until they are proficient in running scenarios.

### 3. Acceptance Criteria
All deliverables will be evaluated by Client. Client reserves the right to withhold final milestone payment until all stakeholders approve model outputs and recommendations.

### 4. Client Responsibilities
Client will provide raw spend files and sales logs in due course.

### 5. Intellectual Property
All code, models, templates, scripts, and documentation developed or utilized during the project will become the exclusive, sole property of Client upon delivery.
