# Statement of Work: Meridian EDA & Prior Calibration Pilot

**Client**: Acme Retail Inc.  
**Provider**: DataSense Consulting LLC  
**Fee**: Milestone-Based Fixed Fee $30,000  
**Timeline**: 4 Weeks  

### 1. Scope of Work
Provider will perform exploratory data analysis and Bayesian prior elicitation on up to 5 marketing channels for historical fiscal years 2024–2025.

### 2. Deliverables & Acceptance Criteria
- **Deliverable 1: Data Audit Report (PDF)**: Summary of missing values, correlation matrix, and spend variance.
  - *Acceptance Event*: Conformance checklist verification within 5 business days of delivery.
- **Deliverable 2: Prior Specification Sheet (YAML)**: Channel priors with documented conversion rates.
  - *Acceptance Event*: 5 business days deemed acceptance window.

### 3. Client Obligations & Timeline Guardrail
Client shall provide CSV data exports per Appendix A within 5 business days of contract execution. Delays in data delivery automatically extend milestone deadlines day-for-day.

### 4. Intellectual Property
Provider assigns final deliverable reports to Client. Provider retains all rights to its pre-existing modeling templates, Python scripts, and proprietary calibration frameworks.
