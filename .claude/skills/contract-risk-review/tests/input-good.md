# Master Services Agreement (MSA) - Standard Balanced Form

### Section 8: Limitation of Liability
8.1 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or lost profit damages.
8.2 The total aggregate liability of either party shall not exceed 100% of the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the 12 months preceding the incident giving rise to liability.

### Section 9: Intellectual Property
9.1 Customer owns all custom deliverables created specifically for Customer under an SOW, effective upon full payment.
9.2 Supplier retains sole ownership of all pre-existing software, models, frameworks, and reusable code libraries. Supplier grants Customer a perpetual license to use embedded background IP.

### Section 11: Termination
11.1 Either party may terminate for cause upon 30 days written notice and failure to cure.
11.2 Customer may terminate for convenience with 45 days written notice, paying for work performed to date.
