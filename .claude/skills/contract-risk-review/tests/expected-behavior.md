# Expected Evaluation Behavior: Contract Risk Review

## Benchmark Criteria

1. **Compliant Input (input-good.md)**:
   - Status: APPROVED or APPROVED_WITH_CONDITIONS
   - Confirms mutual 12-month trailing fee liability cap
   - Verifies explicit background IP retention and fair termination notice

2. **Risky Input (input-risky.md)**:
   - Status: REVISE_AND_RESUBMIT
   - Flags asymmetric liability cap (,000 Customer cap vs uncapped Supplier liability)
   - Flags predatory IP clause forfeiting Supplier pre-existing tools and know-how
   - Flags immediate termination for convenience with refusal to pay for work-in-progress
   - Flags one-sided broad indemnity without defense control or exclusion of customer data
   - Generates exact mutual redline replacement language
