# Commercial Contract Risk Checklist

## 1. Limitation of Liability (LoL)
- [ ] Aggregate liability cap is capped at 100% of 12-month trailing fees paid.
- [ ] Consequential, indirect, and punitive damages are mutually waived.
- [ ] Super-caps (for data breach or IP) are explicitly bounded (e.g. 2x fees or fixed amount), not unlimited.

## 2. Indemnification
- [ ] Indemnity is mutual and limited to third-party IP infringement and gross negligence/willful misconduct.
- [ ] Supplier is NOT indemnifying customer for customer's own data, specifications, or unauthorized modifications.
- [ ] Immediate defense notice, sole right of defense, and settlement approval controls are present.

## 3. Intellectual Property
- [ ] Pre-existing IP, background tools, reusable frameworks, and generalized know-how remain Supplier's sole property.
- [ ] Deliverable IP assignment is strictly contingent upon full payment of relevant invoices.

## 4. Termination & Payment
- [ ] Minimum 30-day written notice and cure period for material breach.
- [ ] If customer terminates for convenience, customer pays for all work performed plus unrecoverable commitments.
- [ ] Late payment interest terms and suspension rights for non-payment > 30 days are included.
