# Standard Protective Replacement Clauses

## 1. Mutual Limitation of Liability Clause
`markdown
Clause 8.1: TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR LOST PROFITS, REVENUE, OR DATA.

Clause 8.2: EACH PARTY’S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL BE LIMITED TO THE TOTAL FEES ACTUALLY PAID OR PAYABLE BY CUSTOMER TO SUPPLIER UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.
`

## 2. Background IP Reservation of Rights
`markdown
Clause 9.3: Supplier retains all right, title, and interest in and to its Pre-Existing Intellectual Property, proprietary tooling, methodologies, templates, and algorithms. Upon full payment of all fees due, Supplier grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, irrevocable, worldwide license to use such Pre-Existing IP solely as embedded within the Deliverables.
`

## 3. Termination for Convenience with Fee Settlement
`markdown
Clause 11.2: Customer may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience upon sixty (60) days prior written notice. Upon such termination, Customer shall immediately pay Supplier for all Services performed, milestones completed, and non-cancellable third-party commitments incurred up to the effective termination date.
`
